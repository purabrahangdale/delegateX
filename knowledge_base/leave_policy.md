# Company Leave Policy

1. Types of Leave:
   - **Annual Leave**: 18 days per calendar year.
   - **Sick Leave**: 12 days per calendar year.
   - **Casual Leave**: 6 days per calendar year.

2. Requesting Leave:
   - Requests must be submitted via the HR Portal at least 5 working days in advance for annual leaves.
   - Sick leave notification must be sent via email or chat to the direct manager before 9:30 AM on the day of absence.

3. Approval Criteria:
   - Leave is subject to manager approval based on active project deadlines and team coverage.
   - A handover document must be prepared for leaves exceeding 3 consecutive days.
