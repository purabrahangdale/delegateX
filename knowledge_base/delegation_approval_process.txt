# Delegation Approval Process Policy

1. Creation:
   - Any Team Lead or Manager can assign a delegation to a direct report.
   - All delegations must have a title, detailed description, priority (Low, Medium, High, Urgent), and a clear deadline.

2. Review & Acceptance:
   - Once assigned, the employee receives a notification and the delegation goes into "Pending" status.
   - The employee must review and transition the task to "In Progress" within 24 hours of assignment.

3. Completion & Submission:
   - When the work is complete, the employee marks the task as "Completed" and inputs the completion date.
   - The assigner will review the work. If approved, the status is closed. If changes are requested, the status is reverted to "In Progress" with comments.
